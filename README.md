## What is is-stupid.lol?

We're building a collection of projects, tools, and communities designed to make the internet a little more interesting.

From forums and personal bios to domains and whatever comes next, everything lives under one growing ecosystem.

No unnecessary complexity. Just internet projects worth using.

## Our philosophy

We believe the internet should be interesting, accessible, and full of things to explore.

We're here to build things, experiment with ideas, and create places people actually want to visit.

The internet doesn't need to be boring.
